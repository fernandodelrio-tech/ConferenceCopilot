THE ASCENDANT WORKS

CONTROLS

Touch — drag anywhere to aim, release to fire. Tap to fire at a point. Two-finger tap or SWAP to change the loaded globe.
Gamepad — right stick aims, A or R2 fires, X swaps, L2 for precision aim, Start pauses.
Keyboard — mouse aims, Space fires, X swaps, Esc pauses.
